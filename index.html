Ortho Appliances
Appliance Type What it does When to use it (indications) Key details
Hawley Appliance Removable

Versatile device, used for:

  • Minor tooth movement
  • Retention
  • Expansion
  • Habit breaking
  • Space maintenance & regaining
  • Bite plane
  • Tongue guard

General-purpose removable appliance:

  • Retention after treatment
  • Minor movements
  • Platform for bite planes, screws, and tongue guards
  • Components:
    • Baseplate (1.5–2.5 mm thick)
    • Retentive parts (Adams/C/Ball clasps, labial bow)
    • Active parts (springs, screw)
  • Acrylic must have relief where teeth move.
Jack Screw Hawley part

Slow, controlled movement of teeth or expansion of the dental arch.

  • Move a group of teeth or a single tooth
  • Palatal expansion
  • As a space regainer to distalize a molar
  • 1 full turn ≈ 1 mm; ¼ turn ≈ 0.25 mm.
  • Two halves move apart on activation.
Anterior Bite Plane Removable

Thickened baseplate on the lingual side of upper anterior teeth; the patient bites on it, discluding posterior teeth.

Deep bite cases — posterior teeth no longer touching, erupt and the deep bite reduces.

  • Works passively by allowing posterior eruption.
  • Part of a Hawley baseplate.
Posterior Bite Plane Removable

Acrylic covering the occlusal surface of posterior teeth, showing cusp-tip indentations of the opposing arch.

  • Anterior crossbite when overbite prevents correction
  • Posterior crossbite — controls vertical dimension during expansion
  • Thickness 1.5–2.5 mm.
  • Helps manage increased vertical dimension from arch expansion.
Tongue Guard (Tongue Crib) Removable

Wire barrier behind the upper anterior teeth that:

  • Controls tongue position
  • Stops tongue thrusting
  • Encourages tongue to move upward
  • Normalizes swallowing
  • Anterior open bite (esp. dental open bite)
  • Tongue-thrust habit
  • Thumb sucking
  • Relapse due to abnormal tongue posture
  • 0.8 mm stainless-steel wire.
  • For open bite, acrylic should NOT cover the lingual side of upper anteriors.
Orthodontic Band Fixed

Thin metal ring cemented around a tooth (usually a molar) acting as a strong anchor for other appliances.

When stronger retention than brackets is needed — to attach:

  • Buccal tubes
  • Hooks
  • Lingual attachments
  • Space maintainers (band & loop, TPA)
  • Steps:
    • Separation (springs ≈ 1 wk)
    • Fitting (patient bites down)
    • Cementation (coat all interior surfaces)
  • Resin-modified cement reduces demineralization.
Lingual Arch Fixed

Wire on the lingual side of the lower teeth connecting the first permanent molars, used for:

  • Space maintenance
  • Prevents mesial molar drift
  • Preserves space
  • Anchorage reinforcement
  • Incisor control
  • Expand/constrict molars
  • Early loss of primary molars
  • Leeway-space preservation
  • Mild lower-arch crowding
  • Mixed dentition
  • Anchorage control
  • ≈ 0.9 mm (0.036″) stainless wire.
  • U-loop mesial to first molars; adapted to touch the cingulum.
Nance Appliance (Nance Holding Arch) Fixed (maxillary)

Holding arch used mainly for anchorage reinforcement and space maintenance.

Maxillary space maintenance and anchorage control (especially molar anchorage).

  • Molar bands connected to a central acrylic button resting on the palate.
Band & Loop Space Maintainer Fixed

Band cemented on a stable tooth (usually posterior to the space); a loop lightly touches the tooth in front, preventing teeth from drifting into the empty space.

Maintaining space after premature tooth loss (e.g., lost primary molar) until the permanent tooth erupts.

  • Loop wire kept very close to gingiva without impinging.
  • Prefabricated version avoids soldering and fits in one appointment (less custom fit).
Distal Shoe Space Maintainer Fixed

Band with a loop and intra-alveolar blade that guides eruption of a permanent first molar.

When a primary second molar is lost before eruption of the permanent first molar — placed at or soon after extraction.

  • 36-mil stainless wire; blade ≈ 1 mm below the mesial marginal ridge of the erupting tooth.
  • Loop contoured close to the ridge.
Transpalatal Arch (TPA) Fixed

Wire across the palate connecting the right and left maxillary molars, used for:

  • Maintains space
  • Anchorage control
  • Molar rotation correction
  • Molar expansion/constriction
  • Molar intrusion
  • Maxillary space maintenance
  • Anchorage reinforcement
  • Molar rotation/expansion/intrusion correction
  • Usually passive but can be activated.
  • Omega loop in the middle for adjustment.
Headgear Extraoral

Applies forces from outside the mouth to teeth and jaws, used to:

  • Distalize maxillary molars
  • Restrict maxillary growth (Class II)
  • Anchorage reinforcement
  • Class II cases
  • Molar distalization
  • Need for extra anchorage
  • Parts:
    • Facebow (inner bow into buccal tubes, outer bow to straps)
    • Force module
    • Anchorage unit (neck strap / head cap)
  • Cervical-pull = backward & downward; high-pull = backward & upward.
Face Mask (Reverse Pull Headgear) Extraoral

Force directed forward and downward; protracts the maxilla and improves skeletal Class III relationship.

Growing patients with maxillary deficiency (skeletal Class III).

  • Anchored to forehead and chin.
